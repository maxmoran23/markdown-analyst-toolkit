# Investigation review: sequential method

## 1. Write the propositions to be decided

Convert the referral into a short list of propositions, each capable of being supported, contradicted, or left unresolved. Include the factual premise, relationship premise, purpose premise, and decision implication separately. For the worked example: a payment occurred; it followed an incoming receipt; the customer says it purchased equipment; the supplied invoice may explain the amount and beneficiary; a later return may reduce unreversed value. None of these alone establishes wrongdoing.

Record the question actually assigned. A bounded review of one payment should not become a complete assessment of the customer's relationship without additional scope and records. Define what would be sufficient to close the analytical question and what would require expanded human review. Do not invent a statutory deadline from the alert date.

## 2. Freeze the source inventory and assess coverage

Read every accessible item and record the processed locator. Reconcile duplicates and unavailable files before constructing the chronology. Assess whether source periods overlap and whether account records are complete. A case built from selected screenshots must be labeled selected evidence; it cannot support a statement that no other activity occurred.

Create a contradiction register immediately. Include conflicting amount, date, identity, status, and purpose information even if a favored explanation appears persuasive. Identify which source is authoritative for which proposition: the payment system for posting status, an original contract for contractual terms, a registry for a legal identity claim, and a witness for what they report having seen. No single source type is universally authoritative.

## 3. Build two timelines

The event timeline describes when relevant activity occurred. The knowledge timeline describes when the analyst or institution obtained information. Separate them when timing affects the interpretation or a later decision. Do not backdate awareness based on a document created earlier but received later.

Sort exact event timestamps using timezone-aware values. If only a date is known, preserve date precision and mark time unknown. When events share a timestamp without sequence data, label order unresolved. Add document and communication events even when they have no monetary amount, but do not include their face amounts in transaction totals.

Each timeline row must link to an evidence item and a precise span. A narrative should be reproducible by reading those rows in order. Avoid dramatic sequencing that implies causation solely from temporal proximity. “Payment followed receipt by one hour” is defensible; “the receipt funded the payment” needs balance and attribution evidence.

## 4. Reconcile funds movement

Sum original posted debits separately from posted returns. Exclude unexecuted instructions from actual flow totals and retain them in attempted-activity analysis if relevant. For each return, match the original transaction reference, currency, amount, and status. Flag overreturns, duplicate credits, or ambiguous linkage.

Calculate `net_unreversed = original_debit - confirmed_linked_returns`, bounded only by validated ledger facts. Do not floor a negative value to zero to hide an overreturn; investigate it. Preserve fees separately. A net unreversed amount is a bookkeeping measure within scope, not a loss determination, criminal-proceeds estimate, or filing amount.

In the example, 11,000.00 USD minus 2,000.00 USD equals 9,000.00 USD. The return fraction is 2,000/11,000 = 0.181818..., displayed as 18.2%. The receipt of 12,000.00 USD plus the return of 2,000.00 USD gives known credits 14,000.00 USD; with debits 11,000.00 USD, known net ledger movement is positive 3,000.00 USD. This does not establish an ending balance because the opening balance is absent.

## 5. Test document consistency without claiming authentication

Compare an invoice's issuer, beneficiary, dates, currency, line items, tax, payment reference, account details, and total with the payment. Separate direct mismatches from unresolved identity bridges. A 10,000.00 USD invoice and an 11,000.00 USD debit differ by 1,000.00 USD; that is observable. The difference might reflect taxes, transport, another invoice, or a discrepancy, but each explanation requires support.

Inspect document completeness: missing pages, unreadable identifiers, absent signature, draft status, or inconsistent version. Do not conclude forgery from formatting irregularities alone. An apparently professional document does not establish delivery or authenticity. Seek corroborating records proportionate to the question, such as contract terms, shipment confirmation, purchase order, or independent beneficiary verification through approved channels.

## 6. Evaluate hypotheses comparatively

For each hypothesis, list facts it predicts, supporting evidence, conflicting evidence, missing discriminating evidence, and the current assessment. Use SUPPORTED only for a proposition actually established within scope; use PATTERN_SUPPORTED when only the observable pattern is established; use UNRESOLVED when alternatives remain; use CONTRADICTED when known facts defeat the stated proposition.

Hypothesis H-01, ordinary equipment settlement, has a compatible profile and customer statement plus an invoice, but the beneficiary and amount do not fully reconcile. It remains unresolved. H-02, unexplained onward movement, is supported as a pattern: the purpose is not yet corroborated, and the debit follows the receipt. It does not prove a crime. H-03, complete reversal, is contradicted by the arithmetic because only 2,000.00 USD of 11,000.00 USD returned.

Do not assign a posterior probability without a defensible model and base rates. A structured hypothesis table is more honest than invented percentages. Evidence can support more than one hypothesis. A missing contract weakens the ability to distinguish alternatives; it does not itself prove the concerning hypothesis.

## 7. Plan discriminating inquiries

Choose the smallest set of inquiries most likely to change the assessment. For the example: establish whether Supplier-Q and Supplier-R are legally or commercially connected; obtain the contract or documentation explaining the full amount; confirm the returned payment's purpose and linkage. These requests directly address the missing premises. A broad search for any negative fact about the customer is less disciplined.

For each proposed inquiry, specify question, item sought, custodian role, approved access path, reason, and result needed. Record not-requested, proposed, authorized, sent, received, and reviewed as distinct states. An assistant's draft request remains proposed. Do not contact subjects or third parties from this package.

If evidence is unavailable, state the remaining uncertainty and the decision it prevents. Recommend a proportionate human next step. Avoid treating silence as admission, unavailable public records as proof of concealment, or a refusal to upload restricted material as suspicious conduct.

## 8. Draft findings with bounded language

Use a precise condition and implication: “The supplied invoice does not reconcile to the payment; beneficiary linkage and 1,000.00 USD remain unexplained.” Cite the rows. Keep observation and inference in separate sentences. Include the strongest counterevidence, such as the compatible business profile and reported explanation.

Use a neutral chronology that answers who, what, when, where, why as understood, and how as observed, while labeling unknowns. Do not write a filing-ready accusation or legal conclusion unless a separate authorized process calls for a draft and the necessary current criteria are established. This suite's output is an investigative assessment, not a submitted report.

## 9. Reassess after new evidence

When new evidence arrives, preserve the prior assessment, add the item to the inventory, update affected chronology and hypothesis rows, and explain the change. Do not delete the original discrepancy merely because it is later resolved. A resolution should state the new evidence and why it answers the earlier question.

Recompute monetary measures after a corrected posting or additional linked return. Revisit dependent findings when a source is withdrawn or shown incomplete. A case can become less concerning without every input becoming complete; a supported explanation for one payment does not automatically explain another.

## 10. Deliver for decision

Present the supported facts, unresolved propositions, contradictory evidence, proposed inquiries, and limitations in a form an independent reviewer can challenge. Reserve final legal, filing, account, reimbursement, or disciplinary decisions for the accountable human process. Record the actual decision only after it exists, with its authority and rationale.
