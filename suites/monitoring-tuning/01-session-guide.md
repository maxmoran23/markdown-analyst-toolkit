# Session guide — Monitoring Scenario and Threshold Review

The central question is not which threshold creates the smallest queue. It is which predeclared candidate has a defensible coverage and workload tradeoff under the supplied evidence, after the monitoring pipeline has preserved the activity that should reach review. Compare raw detection with routed review separately. A nominal improvement in recall can be defeated by suppression, and a reduction in case count can come from merging rather than a lower false-positive rate. The worked case deliberately includes each mechanism so they cannot be treated as interchangeable.

The fixture is a 36-unit customer-window evaluation with three segments and three protected partitions. Four candidates share the same scores, eligibility and labels. Twenty-nine labels are mature within the fiction; seven are unavailable or censored. Eighteen dependence groups contain pairs of units. G17 is suppressed without supplied current authority. The complete below-threshold frame is retained alongside a six-record illustrative review draw. No statistical population inference, live effectiveness claim or deployment authorization is part of this specimen.

## Operating agreement for this attachment set

This is a manual analytical session. Work only with the records the user has lawfully supplied and the public context you actually retrieve. The instruction set defines a reproducible method, not an institution’s policy. Do not fill an absent policy with an industry-sounding default. Do not infer the user’s authority from their familiarity with the topic. When a required decision or input is absent, continue the independent analytical work and identify the exact unresolved gate. A missing approval is a factual state, not an invitation to manufacture a reviewer or pause every calculation.

Start by separating the case’s question from the desired file format. A request for an attractive dashboard is not evidence that the population, outcome labels, analytical method or conclusion is established. State the decision question in one sentence, identify the population to which the answer will apply, and name what the supplied material cannot answer. Preserve that scope sentence through the report, workbook and dashboard. If the scope changes, retain the old version and make the changed population and changed answer visible.

Use ten working attachments, files 01 through 10. README explains the package and file 11 records the build; neither substitutes for substantive instructions. Read the complete method, decision rules, schema, quality review and worked example before rendering outputs. A short context window is a capability limitation: request the missing attachment or work through a documented session handoff. Do not silently discard later sections, truncate a register, or claim to have followed an unread instruction. The worked example is a fully populated fictional rehearsal. Real case facts replace it; they do not get blended into it.

## Roles and decision rights

The analyst prepares evidence, calculations, alternatives and recommendations. A source owner explains the origin, scope, exclusions and extraction controls for a supplied dataset. A reviewer challenges the logic and verifies results using evidence that can contradict the analyst. An accountable decision maker accepts, rejects or restricts a proposed action under the applicable governance. These are roles, not invented people. One person may occupy more than one role in a supplied process; record that arrangement and its consequences rather than treating a job title as proof of objectivity.

The assistant may calculate, reconcile, draft and identify contradictions. It does not authorize a production configuration, close a historical case, accept a material limitation, select a legally sufficient retention period, send a referral, or decide whether reporting is required. Record proposed actions with unassigned owners or explicitly proposed role owners when no assignment exists. A populated action row is not an executed action. An empty due date means no due date was supplied; do not invent one to make a dashboard look complete.

## Session sequence and stopping points

At intake, inventory the attachments and record their apparent dates and scopes. At framing, define the population grain, source cutoff, legal or policy context if actually supplied, exclusions and requested output. At evidence review, reconcile control totals and log missing, duplicate, contradictory and unavailable material. At analysis, execute the fixed methods with explicit numerators and denominators. At challenge, test competing explanations and boundary cases. At rendering, copy the same validated facts into all deliverables. At handoff, state what has been checked, what remains unresolved, and the specific human decisions still required.

A blocker to one branch is not a blocker to every branch. Missing operational labels prevents an effectiveness estimate but does not prevent a source-count reconciliation. Missing a change approval prevents a deployment recommendation from being treated as authorized but does not prevent a candidate comparison. Missing archived support prevents a completed historical review of that unit but does not erase other received records. Split the work into calculable, conditionally interpretable and blocked components. Give each blocked component an evidence request with a reason and a consequence for the conclusion.

Do not collapse all exceptions into a generic caveat. A materially incomplete population affects the denominator. A faulty implementation affects which records are detected. A biased evaluation sample affects generalization. A conflicted review process affects the credibility of challenge. A missing approval affects what may happen next. These defects require different corrections. Preserve them separately even when they share an owner or appear in the same row. A single disclaimer at the end cannot repair a favorable headline that ignores these distinctions.

## Evidence discipline inside the session

Use Observed for a result directly supported by inspected supplied material or reproducible calculation. Use Reported for a source’s assertion, including an official document’s description of its own context. Use Inferred for a reasoned conclusion that goes beyond the observed material and names its assumptions. Use Estimated for a modeled or approximate quantity with the method and uncertainty identified. A deterministic count calculated from a complete fictional register is Observed within the fixture; it is not an observed fact about a real customer or institution.

Every material finding needs the affected population, observed fact, applicable criterion, evidence locator, consequence, counterevidence, uncertainty, recommendation and human decision required. A criterion may be a supplied policy, an explicit specification or an illustrative test rule. Label which one it is. A public source provides context only to the extent its scope actually fits. Do not turn a recommendation into a legal mandate or label an internal target as a regulatory threshold. Where the source has been revised, retain the historical applicability date and review the current authority separately.

## Capability checks and delivery alternatives

At the start, determine whether the session can read all attachments, calculate on complete tables, create downloadable files and reopen them. Do not promise a Word document merely because a Markdown report can be drafted. If direct file creation works, follow files 06 through 08 exactly and verify the resulting files. If it does not, supply complete copy-ready text and tables with the required headings and column order. Label that delivery honestly. Never send the user terminal commands, package installation instructions or repository setup as a prerequisite for using this suite.

The optional Python blocks are an in-session calculation and document route, not an instruction for the end user to program the analysis. If execution is unavailable, perform transparent arithmetic in tables and mark any unperformed executable check as NOT_TESTED. Do not print a successful test statement without performing the underlying check. If a renderer cannot preserve a long register legibly, use continuation pages or a companion workbook; do not shrink the font until evidence becomes unreadable. Preserve all rows and state which artifact carries the authoritative detailed register.

A dashboard is a view of the same case, not a second analytical engine. Its total population, filtered population and unknown-state counts must remain distinguishable. A filtered chart cannot silently replace a full-scope denominator. Labels and text must carry meaning independently of color. Preserve the supplied light and dark themes, the offline behavior, print access and visible fictional-data banner while the specimen data remains. Avoid links that imply a live feed or automatic monitoring when this is a manually supplied snapshot.

## Checkpoint and handoff packet

Before ending a session, record the suite version, case ID, scope version, as-of date, source inventory, control totals, completed methods, unresolved exceptions, calculated metrics, proposed actions and next human decisions. Include the exact current register or a complete transferable representation, not a summary that loses excluded or unresolved records. Preserve stable IDs across sessions. A continuation assistant must be able to identify which conclusions were supported, which were proposals, and which calculations need rerunning after new evidence arrives.

A resumed session begins by checking whether any source, predicate, label, scope boundary or decision has changed. Compare old and new values and report the delta before reusing a prior conclusion. New evidence may resolve a limitation, introduce a contradiction or change the denominator. It does not retrospectively make the earlier session complete. Keep the earlier record with its cutoff. A revised conclusion should identify why it changed and which artifacts were regenerated so that a reviewer can reconstruct the analysis at each point in time.

## Assigned responsibilities for this process

### Scenario owner

Supply the stated detection purpose, unit construction, current comparator and score version. Explain why each segment threshold is considered; do not use the evaluation labels to rewrite the purpose after seeing performance.

### Data and label steward

Provide complete feature, label and maturity ledgers. Keep UNKNOWN separate from CENSORED, identify dependence groups, and retain labels learned after the cutoff as later evidence rather than leaking them into protected results.

### Routing owner

Explain deduplication, merge, suppression, cooldown and case-enrichment behavior. Supply the scope and expiry of every exclusion. A suppressed positive requires a route-level explanation, even when raw threshold recall looks favorable.

### Independent reviewer

Reperform candidate matrices, incremental IDs, segment effects and workload. Challenge whether the protected set was really untouched and whether known labels were selected because a previous system alerted them.

### Human change authority

Decide on permitted use only after evidence, coverage, implementation and capacity gates are explicit. The toolkit provides no default acceptance threshold and does not make deployment changes.
