# Worked example — Monitoring Scenario and Threshold Review

## Complete fictional population

SYN-001 contains eight fictional, independently named customer-week units. Scores are supplied fixture values on a 0–100 scale. They were not generated by a production model. POSITIVE and NEGATIVE are synthetic review labels for a defined review-worthy pattern; UNKNOWN means no label was assigned. Distinct group IDs simplify this example and do not demonstrate real-world sample independence.

## Configurations and expected alerts

Each candidate alerts when score is greater than or equal to its threshold. BASE60 is the illustrative baseline. HIGH75 raises the cutoff. LOW50 lowers it. Illustrative handling capacity is five unique alerts for this eight-unit period; it is a scenario assumption, not an operational fact.

BASE60 alerts U01,U02,U03,U04,U07. HIGH75 alerts U01,U02,U07. LOW50 alerts U01,U02,U03,U04,U05,U07. U08 never alerts and remains unknown. The equality boundaries are U04 at 60 and U05 at 50.

## Incremental trade-offs

HIGH75 removes U03, a known positive, and U04, a known negative. Total workload falls by 2/5=40.0%, while labelled recall falls by one of three known positives, or33.3 percentage points. Precision stays50.0%. LOW50 adds U05, a known positive; total workload rises by 1/5=20.0% and exceeds the illustrative capacity by one alert. It captures all three known positives, but unknown U08 could still be a missed positive.

If handling time is assumed to be30 minutes per alert, estimated workload is 2.5,1.5,3.0 hours, respectively. Capacity five at that assumption is 2.5 hours. These estimates use identical handling time solely for illustration. The additional case may require a different time in real review.

## Unknown-label sensitivity

For BASE60, U07 negative/U08 positive yields full-fixture recall2/4=50.0%; U07 positive/U08 negative yields3/4=75.0%. Both unknowns negative yields2/3=66.7%; both positive yields3/5=60.0%. This explicit enumeration is a conditional scenario analysis, not a confidence interval. HIGH75's analogous bounds are25.0%–50.0%; LOW50's are75.0%–100%.

## Expected findings and decision

MT-F01 is HIGH severity, HIGH confidence, Observed: HIGH75 loses U03. MT-F02 is MEDIUM severity, HIGH confidence, Observed: LOW50 produces six alerts against assumed capacity five. MT-F03 is HIGH severity, HIGH confidence, Observed: two units lack labels; performance for the full population is unresolved. SYN-001 supports the arithmetic, while the public references support only methodological context.

The draft recommendation is to investigate LOW50 further with independent labels, representative below-threshold review, and a capacity plan. The fixture does not justify production deployment or establish that BASE60 is adequate. A human decides whether any candidate merits a controlled validation exercise. No alert is closed and no filing decision follows from this comparison.

## Edge cases

At threshold101, no unit alerts; precision is null, known-label recall0/3=0%, and the zero-alert count is not evidence of safety. If all positive labels are removed, recall is null rather than 100%. If U03 shares G01 and overlapping activity with U01, reconsider independence and any uncertainty method. If a later label changes U08 to POSITIVE, recompute all metrics using the dated label version and preserve prior results. If a source row is unreadable, reconcile it as unknown coverage instead of treating it as a nonalert.
