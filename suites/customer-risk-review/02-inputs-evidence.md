# Inputs and evidence — Customer Risk Review

## Minimum customer profile

Obtain a fictional or public-source relationship description that names a stable subject ID, entity type, proposed product, activity purpose, delivery channel, ownership/control structure, relevant geographic connections, and review period. Distinguish incorporation, residence, operations, counterparty location, and payment route. “International” alone is not a usable geography description. Record anticipated activity separately from observed activity, including currency, gross or net basis, cadence, and the period used to calculate each amount.

Request the supplied factor framework with its scale, weights, band boundaries, null behavior, exceptions, and authorized review process. If no framework is supplied, use a qualitative evidence assessment or the explicitly fictional exercise. Do not quietly turn a generic industry list into a purported institution policy. For a repeat review, retain the previous input values, source dates, framework version, and recorded decision so that changes can be decomposed into evidence changes, method changes, and reviewer choices.

Evidence completeness requires a declared checklist before counting. The example uses four items per customer: identity description, ownership/control description, relationship purpose, and source-of-funds explanation. These are exercise evidence categories, not a comprehensive legal onboarding checklist. A “supplied” item means the fictional case contains the stated material; it does not mean an authentic real document was independently verified. Record quality exceptions alongside the completion count rather than treating receipt as sufficiency.

## Controls and adverse information

For a claimed mitigant, request its objective, relevant population, owner role, operation period, test selection method, results, exceptions, and unresolved coverage. A general policy, an attestation, and an independently reproduced operating result have different evidentiary weight. Do not subtract a uniform number from every customer just because the same product is subject to screening. Assess whether the control addresses the particular concern and whether the observations support that claim.

For adverse information, resolve identity and procedural status before using it. Distinguish an allegation, an established finding, a corrected article, and an unrelated namesake. Absence of a public search result is not a certificate of no adverse information. A public-function connection can be relevant context without establishing criminality. Where applicable legal treatment matters, obtain a current authoritative source and jurisdiction-specific interpretation rather than importing a foreign framework unchanged.

## Reviewed public references and applicability

- REF-CDD: [FFIEC Customer Due Diligence overview](https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual/AssessingComplianceWithBSARegulatoryRequirements/02), sections “Customer Risk Profile” and “Ongoing Monitoring,” reviewed Oct 09, 2026. U.S. bank examination context: customer factors are assessed together; the page does not prescribe this suite's categories, scores, or numerical cutoffs. Use current operative law for a real legal determination. This suite does not restate beneficial-owner collection thresholds or exceptions.
- REF-RISK: [FFIEC BSA/AML Risk Assessment](https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual/BSAAMLRiskAssessment/01), sections “Identification” and “Analysis of Specific Risk Categories,” reviewed Oct 09, 2026. Supports considering the institution's actual activities and documenting factor weighting. It supplies no calibration for this fixture's arithmetic.

These were actually read for the stated methodological propositions. The retrieval date describes the author's source check, not a promise that the source will remain unchanged. Later sessions should record their own retrieval status and relevant amendments. No legal obligation follows from a made-up numeric band.

## Evidence classification and intake ledger

Assign every supplied item a stable evidence ID before drawing a conclusion. Record title, issuer, exact page or table locator, retrieval status, publication date, effective date, review cutoff, subject identifier, and the proposition it can support. These attributes belong in the complete saved case record even where the fixed workbook offers fewer columns. The common Evidence sheet follows 07; do not add invented mandatory columns to its schema. A source title is not proof of retrieval, and a search-result snippet is not a substitute for the operative text.

Use Observed for what is directly visible in a record, Reported for a subject's assertion, Inferred for a conclusion drawn from identified observations, and Estimated for a value derived with stated assumptions. Observing a declaration establishes that the declaration exists. It does not independently establish its truth. The fictional fixture is Observed as supplied exercise data while its purported customer statements remain fictional assertions. Public authority references support methodological context; they do not corroborate fictional subjects.

Maintain a manifest of accepted and rejected inputs. Identify duplicate versions, unreadable pages, omitted periods, contradictory statements, and redactions that affect the task. Retain rejected rows with exclusion reasons; do not silently remove awkward evidence. If an attachment is partially readable, name the readable span and preserve a separate unassessed remainder. A count derived from visible rows must not be labelled the full population unless the manifest reconciles it.

Use only public sources, fictional data, or generic permitted material suitable for this personal analytical workflow. Do not request actual account numbers, identity documents, private employment information, privileged advice, or employer records. An analyst who cannot safely supply a material fact can substitute a clearly fictional value for method testing. That substitution supports the exercise only; it cannot support a real disposition. Never claim a fictitious document was obtained from an authority.

## Dates, comparability, and source conflicts

A document can be authentic yet unsuitable for the decision because its entity, period, purpose, or measurement basis differs. Preserve those dimensions before merging evidence. Record both publication and effective dates where available; later publication does not necessarily supersede an earlier operative event. For a historical review, use evidence effective at the historical cutoff and identify later information separately. Do not backfill current knowledge into the historical conclusion without marking the restatement.

When two sources disagree, present each proposition, the difference, and the decisive additional evidence. Authority hierarchy alone cannot fix an entity mismatch or a transcription error. Seek the relevant underlying record and its amendments. If a source is inaccessible, preserve the attempted locator and mark NOT REVIEWED. Do not use a confidence label to conceal unavailable source content. The report should state which uncertainties could change the recommendation and which merely limit descriptive detail.
