# Entity Due Diligence

Build a structured public-source profile of a legal entity: identity, ownership, business model, permissions, material adverse information, and evidence gaps. The pack turns scattered records into an explicit recommendation for further review.

## In plain English

- You bring a specific question and public information you are allowed to use.
- Attach the ten numbered files 01–10 to one AI chat and paste the launch prompt in 01.
- The chat organizes evidence, applies the method, shows gaps, and fills the fixed templates.
- You review the answer and make the decision. The chat does not act on it for you.

Begin with the exact legal entity and contemplated activity. Supply public records or authorized excerpts with dates. A brand, website, or parent company alone is not enough to define the entity being assessed.

## What you receive

A diligence report, ownership-edge table, gap register, and prioritized evidence requests with accountable human decision fields. The report explains the answer, the workbook keeps the supporting records, and the optional HTML dashboard makes the same records easier to review. If the chat cannot create downloadable files, it supplies a complete Markdown report and exact-column tables. You do not need a terminal, automation platform, repository connection, or special agent setup.

## How this was built

The build sequence is: define the decision → verify relevant public references → author the evidence and analytical rules → construct a fictional worked case → specify fixed report/workbook/dashboard contracts → check consistency and review limitations. See 11-build-record.md for recorded checks, actual build stages, and the content-size estimate. Build token consumption and cost are unknown without telemetry; a token estimate of the published text is a content-size estimate, not a bill or a measure of quality. No duration or independent review should be inferred from this description.

## Limits that matter

The pack does not conduct identity verification, certify a beneficial ownership declaration, set a universal risk rating, or approve a relationship. Economic ownership calculations do not determine sanctions status. Guidance was consulted during authoring on 2026-10-09 where explicitly recorded in 02. Refresh changing sources when using the pack. A completed template does not certify compliance or prove the absence of risk.
