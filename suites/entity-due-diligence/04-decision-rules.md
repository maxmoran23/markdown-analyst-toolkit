# Entity Due Diligence — decision rules

## Evidence-driven assessment

| Condition | Assessment | What can be said | Human follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable identifier and dated register align | Identity supported within scope | Subject matches the identified record | Refresh if decision timing requires it |
| Brand maps to several entities | Entity scope incomplete | Roles are not yet allocated | Identify contracting and performing entities |
| Ownership totals below 100.0% | Incomplete ownership map | Residual remains unresolved | Obtain missing holders or documented basis |
| Ownership totals exceed 100.0% | Invalid comparable-basis reconciliation | Calculation cannot be relied upon | Resolve duplicate/mixed-date/class records |
| Complete economic tree but control rights missing | Economic mapping only | Control not assessed | Obtain relevant governance rights |
| Group license belongs to another entity | Permission unresolved | No demonstrated target coverage | Review exact holder and permitted activity |
| Unverified adverse-media report | Reported allegation | Underlying event not established here | Obtain authority/court record and outcome |
| Claimed control lacks operating evidence | Mitigation unproven | Control existence or effectiveness not demonstrated | Seek design and operation evidence |

## Recommendation categories

SUPPORTED FOR THE STATED RESEARCH PURPOSE means the defined factual question has sufficient evidence within the stated limits. It does not approve a relationship. ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE REQUIRED means a material question could be resolved through identified documents. SPECIALIST REVIEW REQUIRED means legal, ownership, technical, or jurisdictional interpretation exceeds the available factual method. NOT ASSESSABLE means essential identity, scope, or evidence is absent. Use more than one category for different questions when necessary.

A consequential recommendation requires a reasoned severity and a separate confidence. HIGH severity can coexist with LOW confidence when an unresolved matter would be important if true; wording must preserve the uncertainty. HIGH confidence in a documented gap is not HIGH confidence that misconduct occurred. Do not use unresolved ownership as a proxy for illicit intent.

## Alternative explanations

An expired license claim can reflect a stale website, a renewed permission not yet supplied, an affiliate structure, an exempt activity, or a real authorization gap. Incomplete ownership disclosure can reflect access restrictions, a public float, layered funds, reporting lag, or concealment; the available evidence must distinguish them. An unusually large activity projection can reflect expansion, seasonality, or aggressive forecasting rather than false reporting. List plausible alternatives and the observation that would discriminate among them.

## No universal threshold

A legal ownership threshold only applies after identifying the jurisdiction, entity and institution scope, activity, applicable provision, effective date, exceptions, and current source. This pack's fictional 10.0% unresolved interest is a completeness observation, not an assertion that every jurisdiction requires every 10.0% holder to be collected. A 25.0% economic interest, a control person, and sanctions ownership propagation answer different questions. Do not substitute one test for another.

## Human disposition

Record the responsible review role and proposed action in Actions. Leave final disposition PENDING until supplied. A reviewer may accept a qualified factual statement, request evidence, seek specialist interpretation, or reject reliance. Record the rationale and evidence version. The pack must not automatically approve an entity, restrict services, make a suspicious-activity determination, or communicate a case to a third party.
