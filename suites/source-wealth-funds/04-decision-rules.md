# Decision rules — Source of Wealth and Funds Review

## Stock and flow rules

| Condition | Treatment | Failure to reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening asset balance | Stock at opening; included once | Treat balance as period income |
| Inherited noncash asset | Wealth addition at supported value; zero cash | Treat inheritance as immediate cash |
| Asset sale | Remove carrying amount; add net cash; isolate gain/loss | Add full proceeds as new wealth and retain sold asset |
| Unrealized appreciation | Wealth valuation change; zero cash | Allocate appreciation to a transfer |
| Own-account transfer | No change in total personal net wealth | Count both debit and credit as income |
| Loan proceeds | Cash with corresponding liability as applicable | Treat borrowing as unencumbered wealth creation |
| Duplicate advice | Preserve source; zero extra economic allocation | Sum every document amount |
| Unknown third-party origin | Separate unsupported allocation | Infer origin from sending institution |

The cash view and net-wealth view must both reconcile, but they need not have equal period movements. A liability-funded cash receipt may increase cash while leaving net wealth unchanged. A noncash gift may increase wealth without increasing cash. A sale below carrying amount may produce positive cash and negative net wealth movement. These distinctions should be explicit in the event classification before arithmetic begins.

## Gross, net, FX, and tax gate

Never compare gross income directly with a net funding requirement without explaining deductions and other commitments. Preserve tax paid, tax withheld, tax estimated, and tax reserved as separate concepts. The fixture provides taxes paid and contains no outstanding tax liability; this simplifying assumption does not apply outside the example. If estimated taxes are used in a scenario, label the assumption and sensitivity rather than certifying compliance.

A missing exchange rate blocks a mixed-currency consolidated total unless an explicitly named scenario rate is supplied. An FX rate must state direction and date. Dividing EUR100,000.00 by 1.20 when the rate is USD per EUR is a unit error. Do not replace a historical conversion with today's rate to make a current value look supported. Preserve a historical wealth view and a separate present-value scenario if both are needed.

## Evidence sufficiency and disposition

A SUPPORTED_ALLOCATION means the supplied exercise contains the amount, entitlement, and proposed connection needed for that allocation. It does not mean the documents are authentic or that lawful origin is proved. UNRESOLVED means a material link is absent or contradictory. DUPLICATE_EXCLUDED means the document supports an already-counted event and contributes zero new funds. A missing numeric amount is null with explanation, never silently zero.

A known unsupported allocation is not the same as a known illicit allocation. State the missing information without accusing the sender or recipient. Confidence can be HIGH that $60,000.00 lacks a supplied explanation, while confidence about its actual economic origin is unavailable. The severity label refers to the review consequence, not a criminal conclusion. A coherent explanation can justify further examination; coherence alone is not corroboration.

## Thresholds and reviewer decisions

This suite supplies no acceptable unexplained-wealth tolerance and no universal documentation percentage. A 90.0% supported allocation is a descriptive result, not a passing grade. A small unexplained amount can be decision-critical depending on the facts, and a large noncash valuation gap may affect different questions from a funds gap. The authorized reviewer must apply relevant policy and law through an appropriate environment.

Where a human proposes proceeding despite an unresolved item, record the proposal, rationale, limits, required evidence, and actual authority status. Do not manufacture approval. Where further evidence changes an event, recalculate the complete wealth bridge, cash balance, funding allocation, metrics, findings, and narrative. Changing a number only in the executive paragraph leaves an inconsistent package and fails QA.
