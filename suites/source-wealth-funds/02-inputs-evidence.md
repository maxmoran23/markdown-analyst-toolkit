# Inputs and evidence — Source of Wealth and Funds Review

## Define the subject, stock, and particular funds

Specify the fictional or publicly described subject, ownership share, review purpose, wealth measurement date, accumulation period, proposed transaction date, amount, currency, and expected recipient. Distinguish personal assets, jointly owned assets, business assets, trust property, and assets controlled without beneficial entitlement. Gross enterprise value is not automatically personal wealth. A family estate's total value is not automatically the beneficiary's distribution.

For the wealth view, obtain opening assets and liabilities, material additions and disposals, income and expenses, valuation changes, and closing balances on compatible dates. For the funds view, obtain the specific economic origin, entitlement, payment chain, dates, net proceeds, and proposed allocation. In a public-source exercise some records may be unavailable; state that limitation rather than requesting private customer bank statements. A fictional analogue can test the method without purporting to resolve an actual case.

## Source-specific evidence expectations

Employment income needs period, gross pay, relevant deductions, net receipts, and whether the amount was received or merely accrued. Business ownership needs the subject's economic entitlement, not just the company's revenue. A dividend needs a distribution event, amount due to this holder, payment date, deductions, and currency. A company valuation cannot be treated as cash personally available to its shareholder.

An inheritance needs the relevant estate or transfer event, subject entitlement, asset type, quantity, transfer date, valuation basis, restrictions, and later disposal if cash funding is claimed. An inherited stock of assets may consist of securities, property, or cash; the word “stock” here describes a balance at a date, not necessarily shares. A sale needs asset ownership, consideration, settlement date, fees, taxes or withholding, debt discharge if any, and net amount received. An executed agreement without settlement evidence can support a contractual claim but not prove available cash.

A loan is a financing source with a corresponding liability unless the facts establish otherwise. A gift needs donor relationship, donor capacity and origin to the extent relevant, transfer, and entitlement; it is not income merely because cash was received. An own-account transfer needs a link to the underlying economic source, not just a reputable sending institution. Unknown receipts remain unexplained; a familiar banking route does not answer their economic origin.

## Currency, taxes, and valuation metadata

Preserve original currency and amount, rate direction, rate date, rate source, and reporting currency. Record gross amount, each deduction, net amount, and whether a deduction is paid, withheld, accrued, estimated, or merely reserved. Do not deduct the same tax both from net proceeds and again as a standalone wealth expense. A tax reserve can remain an owned asset with a separate liability; define its treatment before including it in a bridge. The fixture instead uses explicitly paid fictional taxes to avoid that ambiguity.

Retain valuation basis and date for illiquid assets. A broker price, a property estimate, a contract price, and a net liquidation amount are not interchangeable. Liabilities belong to their obligor and must be matched to the correct share of jointly held assets. If ownership percentage or liability responsibility is absent, withhold the affected net-wealth conclusion rather than assume full entitlement.

## Reviewed public reference and applicability

REF-PEP: [FATF Guidance on Politically Exposed Persons, June 2013](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/content/dam/fatf-gafi/guidance/Guidance-PEP-Rec12-22.pdf), paragraphs 86–94, printed pages 20–21, reviewed Oct 09, 2026. The guidance distinguishes the origin of overall wealth from the origin of particular relationship funds and cautions against stopping at the transferring institution. It addresses PEP-related FATF standards; this suite does not extend every measure to all customers or determine current national law. The accounting bridge, thresholds, fictional tax inputs, and allocations are original teaching methods, not FATF formulas.

The document was actually read for that limited conceptual distinction. Its publication date remains June 2013 even though retrieved in 2026. A real applicability decision needs current authoritative local rules and the relevant customer/account facts. The fixture contains no public official or actual PEP and makes no legal classification.

## Evidence classification and intake ledger

Assign every supplied item a stable evidence ID before drawing a conclusion. Record title, issuer, exact page or table locator, retrieval status, publication date, effective date, review cutoff, subject identifier, and the proposition it can support. These attributes belong in the complete saved case record even where the fixed workbook offers fewer columns. The common Evidence sheet follows 07; do not add invented mandatory columns to its schema. A source title is not proof of retrieval, and a search-result snippet is not a substitute for the operative text.

Use Observed for what is directly visible in a record, Reported for a subject's assertion, Inferred for a conclusion drawn from identified observations, and Estimated for a value derived with stated assumptions. Observing a declaration establishes that the declaration exists. It does not independently establish its truth. The fictional fixture is Observed as supplied exercise data while its purported customer statements remain fictional assertions. Public authority references support methodological context; they do not corroborate fictional subjects.

Maintain a manifest of accepted and rejected inputs. Identify duplicate versions, unreadable pages, omitted periods, contradictory statements, and redactions that affect the task. Retain rejected rows with exclusion reasons; do not silently remove awkward evidence. If an attachment is partially readable, name the readable span and preserve a separate unassessed remainder. A count derived from visible rows must not be labelled the full population unless the manifest reconciles it.

Use only public sources, fictional data, or generic permitted material suitable for this personal analytical workflow. Do not request actual account numbers, identity documents, private employment information, privileged advice, or employer records. An analyst who cannot safely supply a material fact can substitute a clearly fictional value for method testing. That substitution supports the exercise only; it cannot support a real disposition. Never claim a fictitious document was obtained from an authority.

## Dates, comparability, and source conflicts

A document can be authentic yet unsuitable for the decision because its entity, period, purpose, or measurement basis differs. Preserve those dimensions before merging evidence. Record both publication and effective dates where available; later publication does not necessarily supersede an earlier operative event. For a historical review, use evidence effective at the historical cutoff and identify later information separately. Do not backfill current knowledge into the historical conclusion without marking the restatement.

When two sources disagree, present each proposition, the difference, and the decisive additional evidence. Authority hierarchy alone cannot fix an entity mismatch or a transcription error. Seek the relevant underlying record and its amendments. If a source is inaccessible, preserve the attempted locator and mark NOT REVIEWED. Do not use a confidence label to conceal unavailable source content. The report should state which uncertainties could change the recommendation and which merely limit descriptive detail.
