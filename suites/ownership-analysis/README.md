# Ownership Analysis

Build a dated, evidence-linked ownership map and calculate economic interests without confusing them with control rights or jurisdiction-specific legal ownership tests. The pack is designed for transparent analysis of layered entities and incomplete disclosures.

## In plain English

- You bring a specific question and public information you are allowed to use.
- Attach the ten numbered files 01–10 to one AI chat and paste the launch prompt in 01.
- The chat organizes evidence, applies the method, shows gaps, and fills the fixed templates.
- You review the answer and make the decision. The chat does not act on it for you.

Choose the target entity, the effective date, and the ownership basis first. Supply direct holdings with source records. The chat reconciles each entity, traces distinct paths, identifies unresolved branches, and explains which calculations can be relied on under the stated assumptions.

## What you receive

A direct-edge table, ultimate-interest table, path calculations, control-rights discussion, and specific evidence requests for missing or inconsistent branches. The report explains the answer, the workbook keeps the supporting records, and the optional HTML dashboard makes the same records easier to review. If the chat cannot create downloadable files, it supplies a complete Markdown report and exact-column tables. You do not need a terminal, automation platform, repository connection, or special agent setup.

## How this was built

The build sequence is: define the decision → verify relevant public references → author the evidence and analytical rules → construct a fictional worked case → specify fixed report/workbook/dashboard contracts → check consistency and review limitations. See 11-build-record.md for recorded checks, actual build stages, and the content-size estimate. Build token consumption and cost are unknown without telemetry; a token estimate of the published text is a content-size estimate, not a bill or a measure of quality. No duration or independent review should be inferred from this description.

## Limits that matter

A diagram does not prove ownership. The pack does not identify undisclosed holders, certify declarations, resolve every complex trust or circular holding, or assign legal beneficial-owner status from one percentage alone. Guidance was consulted during authoring on 2026-10-09 where explicitly recorded in 02. Refresh changing sources when using the pack. A completed template does not certify compliance or prove the absence of risk.
