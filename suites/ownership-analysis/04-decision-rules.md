## Confidence and materiality

HIGH confidence in a calculation requires known inputs, a supported method, a reproducible path, and no material unresolved overlap or contradiction. It does not make the source schedule independently verified. State “high confidence in arithmetic under reported inputs” where that is the real position. LOW or NOT ASSESSABLE applies when critical percentages, dates, or definitions are missing.

A gap is HIGH materiality when it could change the intended decision or a legally relevant threshold under the actual applicable rule. It is MEDIUM when it affects completeness or interpretation but no decisive effect is demonstrated. LOW is reserved for limited documentation issues. Do not classify a person as high risk merely for appearing in a complex structure; describe the particular unresolved fact and consequence.

## Alternative explanations and contradiction handling

Conflicting holdings can arise from a completed transfer, dilution, share split, different classes, direct versus beneficial reporting, nominee disclosure, rounding, or error. Compare effective dates and bases before deciding one source contradicts another. A later source may be more current but less precise; a historic certified filing may be more appropriate to a historical question. Preserve both and explain the choice.

If two records cannot be reconciled, calculate separate scenarios only when each is internally coherent and clearly labeled. Do not average two disputed percentages to invent a compromise. Identify which fact would resolve the difference. If the intended decision is sensitive to the range, recommend evidence or specialist review rather than choosing the convenient endpoint.

## Human decisions and legal boundaries

No resulting percentage automatically authorizes onboarding, exclusion, disclosure, restriction, sanctions treatment, or regulatory reporting. The pack may recommend further evidence, a revised graph, or specialist interpretation. It must not present legal beneficial-owner status without a named applicable basis and authorized review. Cross-border structures require separate legal analyses, even when the factual graph is shared.

Keep the reviewer decision separate from the analyst's graph state. A human may accept a partial graph for a limited research purpose while requiring additional evidence for a consequential use. Record the purpose, limitations accepted, unresolved items, evidence version, and actual reviewer. Do not imply acceptance where the decision field is still PENDING.
