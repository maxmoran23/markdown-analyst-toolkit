# Analysis method — Jurisdiction Risk and Exposure Review

## 1. Define geography as a relationship, not a label

Write the exposure question in one sentence with its role and denominator. “What proportion of supplied outgoing transfer value has beneficiary jurisdiction J-D?” is well formed. “How risky is J-D exposure?” is incomplete until the activity, time, population, and meaning of risk are specified. A decision may require several questions, but they should be answered separately before synthesis.

Assign stable identifiers to the economic event and each relevant party. Keep distinct roles even if their country values happen to match. A party incorporated in one place can operate elsewhere; an intermediary can route a payment without owning the funds. Do not infer customer nationality from a surname, language, bank location, or routing code. Use a supplied public or fictional fact and state the type of nexus.

## 2. Freeze the cutoff and classify source status

State the business period, review cutoff, and source retrieval date. The source may describe a condition effective before its publication or a measure effective after announcement. Match the operative period to the question instead of selecting whichever date is latest. For a historical exposure review, retain the historical classification in effect at that time and show subsequent changes separately.

Distinguish a primary source actually read from an index page pointing to an unread statement. The index may establish the existence of a document but not every condition within it. Preserve relevant text locators and the exact document title in the saved record. If a dynamic page changed during the session, identify which version supported the conclusion and whether a recheck is required. Avoid an unsupported “current as of today” label.

## 3. Keep the four analytical dimensions separate

First, record strategic AML/CFT/CPF context using the exact category stated by the relevant authority. Second, record sanctions scope as an applicability question involving rules and facts. Third, record governance or corruption context with measurement limitations. Fourth, record the actual customer or transaction connection. The first three describe different aspects of jurisdiction context; the fourth links that context to the reviewed activity.

Do not average these dimensions into a score unless a supplied permitted methodology defines the transformation and its limitations. Even then, preserve a separate legal-scope assessment because a composite average cannot decide whether a transaction is prohibited. A low corruption estimate cannot cancel a relevant restriction. A strategic-deficiency category does not establish that an identified customer's funds are criminal. An unexplained payment can merit review even when the jurisdiction has no fixture category.

## 4. Build an exposure ledger that can be reconciled

Determine one primary allocation role for additive totals. The fixture uses beneficiary jurisdiction, one destination per unique transfer. Include an UNKNOWN destination bucket. Sum the full transfer value across the role, not just mapped records. Require a currency for every amount. If mixed currencies exist, keep separate totals until a declared conversion basis is supplied; do not add numeric amounts with different units.

Where FX is permitted, record original amount, currency, rate convention, rate date, source, and converted amount in the checkpoint. A rate labelled USD per unit is multiplied by the original amount; units per USD requires division. Explain whether daily spot, monthly average, or a scenario rate fits the exposure question. Revaluation changes can alter monetary shares without any new activity, so separate them from flow changes in period comparisons.

## 5. Calculate role-specific shares with explicit missingness

For each primary destination, sum included transfer amounts and divide by total included value. The fixture total is $1,000,000.00 across eight transfers. J-A has $150,000.00, J-B $250,000.00, J-C $250,000.00, J-D $250,000.00, and UNKNOWN $100,000.00. Their shares are 15.0%, 25.0%, 25.0%, 25.0%, and 10.0%. The sum is 100.0% because this one role is mutually exclusive and complete with the unknown bucket.

If a report additionally shows mapped-only concentration, name its denominator. J-D's mapped-only share is $250,000.00/$900,000.00 = 27.8%; its full-population destination share remains 25.0%. The mapped-only figure must not replace the overall exposure statement. Unknown geography can materially alter allocation, and its exclusion can overstate apparent certainty about concentration.

Counts and amounts can also tell different stories. J-D receives one of eight transfers, or 12.5% by count, yet 25.0% by value. Neither is the “correct” universal risk metric. Explain the decision each measure informs and retain the common population. Avoid presenting high monetary concentration as proof that most counterparties or customers share the same nexus.

## 6. Deduplicate overlapping routes and other roles

For an any-nexus measure, form the set of unique economic-event IDs satisfying at least one selected role. Sum each event once. J-D is T03's intermediary and T06's destination. The union is {T03,T06}, worth $400,000.00, or 40.0% of the full value. The destination amount remains $250,000.00. The additional $150,000.00 is route exposure, not additional payment volume.

A union across jurisdictions may itself overlap. J-A appears as an intermediary on seven transfers, totalling $850,000.00, and as beneficiary on T01 and T07, which are already in those seven. J-A's any-nexus union is therefore $850,000.00, not $1,000,000.00. Adding all jurisdictions' any-nexus shares can exceed 100% legitimately because one payment can touch several places. Label such measures nonadditive and never use their sum as total transfer value.

## 7. Evaluate relevance and alternatives

For each contextual concern, describe the plausible exposure mechanism and what evidence supports it. A known destination may matter to the service or customer activity, while a technical routing location may warrant a different question. Neither is automatically irrelevant or determinative. Examine role, product, sector, parties, timing, purpose, and the applicable authority. Keep legal interpretation separate from exposure mapping.

Consider benign and adverse alternatives. T03's J-D intermediary might be a routine settlement route; it might also bring a relevant program question depending on facts absent from the fixture. The ledger establishes that J-D is on the supplied route. It does not establish which alternative applies. Identify the narrow evidence that would resolve the question rather than escalating every route in identical language.

## 8. Use scenario bounds without inventing country status

Allocate UNKNOWN exposure hypothetically to test sensitivity. If all $100,000.00 of T05 belonged to J-D, destination exposure would be $350,000.00 or 35.0%. If none belonged to J-D, it stays 25.0%. This 25.0%–35.0% range is a deterministic allocation bound under the fixture, not a statistical confidence interval. Do not assign a midpoint or imply all destinations are equally probable.

For category concentration, J-B and J-C together hold $500,000.00 by destination under their distinct fictional categories. A combined “selected AML-context categories” share can be stated as 50.0% if both categories and the rationale are named; it must not be called a sanctions share or an illicit-flow estimate. If unknown T05 entered either selected category, that descriptive share would reach 60.0%. Report the categories separately first.

## 9. Produce targeted human questions

A useful next action names the missing fact, affected exposure, relevant authority, reviewer role, and consequence for the draft conclusion. For T05, request the beneficiary-jurisdiction mapping and source basis. For T03 and T06, request party and sector facts needed to assess the fictional J-D scope question. For the context snapshots, request refreshed applicable primary statements if moving beyond the exercise.

The conclusion should preserve both value and count, known and unknown, destination and route, and context and legal applicability. Recommend a proportionate review sequence without instructing an operational system to block, reject, approve, or exit anyone. A public-source research artifact remains a draft until an authorized human applies the relevant decision process.
