# Correspondent Banking

Review respondent-bank relationships, nested access and payment-information gaps using a bounded, risk-based evidence record. The intended decision is: **Which relationship-specific transparency and due-diligence gaps need authorized follow-up before a human disposition?** This suite supplies a method for a human reviewer; it does not approve a relationship, establish misconduct, or execute an operational action.

## Start here

Open 01-session-guide.md and choose Full workflow, Example check, Analyze, or Render. Its stage table is the authoritative attachment guide. In ordinary full mode, attach files 01–10 and the permitted case inputs to one AI chat, then copy its launch prompt. README and 11-build-record.md are browsing material. If attachment or context capacity is limited, use the stages in 01 and preserve the complete case record between them.

The unit of relationship review is the respondent bank; payment records support particular questions about its services and behavior. The suite distinguishes disclosed nested access, unexpected underlying institutions, payable-through arrangements and ordinary treasury payments. It does not presume that every customer of a respondent requires a separate correspondent-level customer file or that an unfamiliar jurisdiction warrants exit.

## Interpretation limits

All example people, organizations, documents, transactions and identifiers are invented. Public references explain a method and its applicability; they do not substantiate any fictional case. A link is a research lead until the current session actually reviews it. Dates and source status in 02 describe the authoring review, not a guarantee that the material remains current. Local rules, approved criteria and reviewer authority must be established for a real decision.

This pack was assembled by defining the decision, checking selected primary references, writing evidence and interpretation rules, constructing a complete fictional population, and aligning the fixed outputs. File 11 records checks actually performed. Content-size estimates are not measured build consumption, cost or proof of quality. A successful fictional example proves only the stated exercise was reproduced.
