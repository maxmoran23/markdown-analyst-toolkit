# Adverse Media — inputs and evidence

## Define the review before interpreting a result

Supply the canonical subject, stable identifiers, relevant jurisdictions, original-script names where available, decision purpose, event window and knowledge cutoff. Separate when conduct allegedly occurred from when an article was published and when the session retrieved it. A 2026 article can repeat a 2019 event; an old event may have a new material outcome. Neither freshness nor age alone establishes relevance.

| Input | Minimum useful content | Missing-input treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Legal name, jurisdiction and reliable identifier | Name-only matches remain unresolved |
| Decision use | Review, clarification, risk reassessment or citation | Do not infer an approval or exit mandate |
| Source population | Items received, planned searches and access failures | Bound completion to the recorded plan |
| Event scope | Conduct categories, relevant roles and period | Explain exclusions before reading outcomes |
| Procedural documents | Complaint, notice, order or docket excerpt with locator | Keep reported status distinct from verified status |
| Subsequent account | Correction, appeal, dismissal, denial or clarification | Record not supplied rather than assume none exists |
| Language | Original and translated text, reviewer competence | Preserve material ambiguities for review |

## Public reference and applicability

REF-NNS: [Wolfsberg Group, Negative News Screening FAQs](https://db.wolfsberg-group.org/assets/b3652a98-4bc6-4b42-9f05-e378a4adbd95/Negative%20News%20Screening%20FAQs%20(2022).pdf), 2022, with [publication notice dated 2022-05-11](https://wolfsberg-group.org/news/publication-of-the-negative-news-screening-faqs/). Primary publisher PDF, including Q4–Q5, and publication notice reviewed during authoring on 2026-10-09. This is industry guidance, not legislation or a universal requirement. Its relevance here is the risk-based selection of adverse information, source credibility and differentiation of event stages. The suite's exact registers, states, illustrative calculations and review gates are original conventions. The current user must establish local legal requirements and the permitted purpose before applying them.

Public method references cannot prove subject facts. Use the issuing authority's operative record for procedural status where accessible. A prosecutor's release, a reporter's summary and a court's judgment have different evidential roles even when all are accurately quoted. A notice that charges were filed does not establish that those charges were proved. A final civil or administrative order must be described within its own scope, including admissions, findings, settlements, remedies and appeal status actually stated.

## Evidence record and lineage

Assign E-001 onward to real evidence, SYN- identifiers to the fictional example, and REF- identifiers to public method references. The full case record should retain title, publisher, exact locator, original language, event date, publication date, actual retrieval time, access state, source family, subject identifiers, supported proposition and limitations. Map these details into the exact common Evidence fields in 07 without inventing new mandatory columns. A URL alone does not show that text was reviewed.

Source families describe information origin. A wire report, ten reprints, and a blog quoting the wire are one origin for the copied assertion. The same article can contain two origins: a copied allegation and independently inspected documents. Record the proposition-level lineage in assessment_note; do not force all of its claims into a single corroboration count. If the origin cannot be established, say unknown rather than assume independence.

Prefer exact passages to search-result snippets. Retain the nearby qualifier when the operative sentence says 'alleged', 'denied', 'dismissed', 'without admission', or 'subject to appeal'. Keep the source's role description: accused party, witness, victim, adviser and associated company are not interchangeable. When translations differ materially, retain both versions, explain the disputed word and defer the affected judgment to a competent reviewer.

## Identity and relevance gates

Compare identifiers before names. A registration number mismatch is not overcome by a perfect text-name match. Evaluate name changes, mergers and successor entities with dated linkage evidence. A shared officer does not transfer every allegation between companies; a shared address does not identify one legal person. Preserve context-only relationships separately from attributed events. Do not expand the subject through relatives or social associations without a decision-relevant, permitted basis.

A positive identity match answers who the source concerns. It does not answer whether the allegation is true. A relevant conduct category answers why the event deserves review. It does not determine severity or action. Record all three judgments separately so a later reviewer can change one without corrupting the others.

## Missing, restricted and corrective material

Maintain a planned-search ledger with question, source category, language, date window, query or supplied-input locator, result count, access status and limitation. Treat a paywall, absent attachment or unavailable docket as an access limit. Do not bypass restrictions or infer a favorable outcome. A completed search yielding no records is bounded by the exact source and query; it is not a certificate of no adverse information.

Record a subject's available public response alongside the allegation, identifying its date and the proposition addressed. No response supplied does not mean the subject declined an opportunity to respond. The analysis may recommend an authorized clarification process, but must not contact the subject itself. Preserve retractions and corrections prominently. Their receipt requires reassessment of every dependent finding, even when the original reporting remains accessible.
