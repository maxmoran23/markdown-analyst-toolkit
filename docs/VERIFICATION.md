## Not performed or not established

- No enterprise Copilot tenant, license, attachment limit or organizational permission was tested.
- No controlled study of model accuracy, instruction adherence or production detection effectiveness was performed.
- No native Microsoft Word/Excel rendering, native formula recalculation, actual browser print output, screen-reader audit or exhaustive device matrix was tested.
- No real customer, operational transaction, live sanctions target or non-public case was used.
- No observed per-suite generation-token or monetary-cost allocation is available.
- Not every alternate narrative has a separate executable test. The delivered checks identify the rows and propositions they actually reproduce.

Illustrative process QA may show an arithmetic PASS while human review, deployment,
acceptance or disposition remains unrecorded. Those are different propositions.
A library test never supplies a missing reviewer or converts the user's case into an
approved result. Apply file09 to the actual analysis and artifacts in the current session.
